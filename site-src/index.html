The problem

Proving one payment shouldn't cost you all of them.

Shops, exchanges and auditors ask for a viewing key today. ZIP 311 left Orchard's payment disclosure as a TODO, so there was nothing smaller to hand over.

A viewing key

  • ✕Every incoming payment, past and future
  • ✕Your balance and who paid you
  • ✕Can't be taken back once shared
  • ✕Doesn't prove who sent a payment

A Zceipt receipt

  • ✓One payment: recipient, amount, memo
  • ✓Address and balance stay hidden
  • ✓Proves your key made the payment
  • ✓Anyone can check it, offline, forever
How it works

Pay as usual. Share a link.

Pay from a shielded wallet

Nothing changes about the payment. Orchard or Ironwood, any amount, any memo.

Make a receipt

Your wallet makes a fresh Halo 2 proof for that one transaction and signs it. A viewing key can prepare it; the seed only signs.

Anyone verifies it

The link opens in a browser, which fetches the transaction from the chain and checks the proof on the device. No account, no server to trust.

For developers

Take shielded payments without a viewing key.

  • Payment API. Proof requests, signed webhooks, refunds, attestations.
  • SDKs. TypeScript, Python, Rust and Go.
  • HTTP 402 gate. Express, Next.js, FastAPI, Django.
  • Wallets. Swift and Kotlin bindings, a zcash-devtool patch.
