<Warning>
This proof uses a **new Halo 2 circuit** with a **ported BLAKE2b gadget**. The gadget's authors
say theirs is unaudited, and the port is too. It has run on a private regtest chain, not on
testnet. See [forked circuits](/reference/forked-circuits).
</Warning>

[Payer tags](/composability/distinct-payers) tell wallets apart by account. A donor with two
accounts of one wallet would get two tags and count twice. A sibling-account proof closes that
gap.

## Use

```bash
receipts siblings -w <wallet> -i <identity> --accounts 0,1 --domain round-2026 --out s.txt
receipts verify @s.txt --domain round-2026
```

The output lists the two tags the proof is about. With payer tags from real payments (each tag
tied to its payment by the payer-tag proof), a verifier learns the two payments came from accounts
of one wallet.

## How it works

Orchard account keys are hardened ZIP 32 children of the wallet's coin-level key:

```text
I  = BLAKE2b-512("Zcash_ExpandSeed", c_par || 0x81 || sk_par || i)
sk = I[..32]
nk = ToBase(BLAKE2b-512("Zcash_ExpandSeed", sk || 0x07))
tag = Poseidon(nk, domain)
```

The circuit takes private `(sk_par, c_par)` and indices `i_1`, `i_2`, computes both children's
`nk` with BLAKE2b, hashes them to tags, and checks them against public `tag_1`, `tag_2` and
`domain`. The parent key, the indices and the `nk`s stay private.

So a tag can't be claimed for another wallet's account without a BLAKE2b or Poseidon collision.
A test (`against_orchard::accounts_are_children_of_the_coin_key`) checks that the in-circuit
derivation equals the `orchard` crate's.

## Cost

BLAKE2b runs inside the circuit: four compressions, so the circuit is `k = 17` (the gadget's
16-bit XOR table). Proving takes about 72 seconds on the development machine, far larger than
an Action proof. The parameters are cached at `~/.cache/receipts/kce-params-17.bin` and checked
by digest.

## Limits

- Hardened derivation only, so it covers accounts of one seed, not independently generated
  wallets.
- Unaudited gadget and port.

Code: `crates/receipts-kce`. The regtest end-to-end run ties a sibling proof to a real payer tag.
