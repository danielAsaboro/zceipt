How this stays private

Every contribution is a shielded Zcash payment whose sender proved it with a zero-knowledge receipt. This page shows only a rounded total and a count, after enough people have given, refreshed in batches. No amounts, transactions or times of individual gifts are shown, so nobody can match a gift to a person. Notes appear only if a contributor chose to sign one.
