---
title: "How to check a receipt you were sent"
description: "Verify a receipt in the browser or with zceipt verify, and confirm it paid you."
---

This guide checks a receipt someone sent you and confirms the payment went to your address.

## In the browser

1. Open the link. If you were sent a file or the text itself, open
   [zceipt.xyz/verify](https://zceipt.xyz/verify/), paste it into **Receipt or link** (or use
   **Open a file**) and press **Verify receipt**.
2. Read the card: the amount, the recipient, the memo and the signed message. The stamp says
   **VERIFIED · RECIPIENT NOT CHECKED** until you check the recipient.
3. Under **Was it paid to you? Your address**, paste your receiving address and press
   **Check**. The stamp turns to **PAID TO YOU**, or **VALID · NOT TO YOUR ADDRESS**.

The page fetches the payment's block from a chain server. To fetch nothing, open
**Server, or offline** and paste the raw transaction and its height instead.

## In the terminal

```bash
zceipt verify @receipt.txt --expect-address "$(cat merchant.ua)"
```

On the real testnet receipt from the [terminal guide](/use/terminal):

```text
(paid 0.01000000 TAZ to the expected address)
VALID receipt
  network   testnet
  tx        ec78e6b2…c717b493 (mined at height 4550523)
  pool      Ironwood
  message   Order 1093
  sender    proved spend authority for action(s) [1]
  paid      0.01000000 TAZ to utest14te2vs…
            memo: Order 1093: two coffees
  nothing else about the sender's wallet is revealed
  mined     281 confirmations on testnet.zec.rocks:443
```

Add `--min <ZEC>` to also require an amount, and `--challenge <hex>` if you asked for the
payment with a request.

## When it doesn't pass

| Stamp | Means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| NOT VALID | The receipt was changed after it was made, or wasn't made by the wallet that paid. | Don't rely on it. |
| NOT PROVEN BY THE PAYER | The payment is on chain, but this receipt came from someone who only saw it. | Don't treat it as proof of who paid. |
| VERIFIED · NOT FINAL YET | Valid, but the block has few confirmations. | Check again later. |
| NOT A RECEIPT | The link was cut off, or what was pasted isn't a receipt. | Ask for the whole link, or a file. |
| COULD NOT CHECK | The chain server couldn't be reached, or doesn't have the transaction yet. | Try again, or pick another server under **Server, or offline**. |

What the verifier learns, and what it doesn't, is on
[what a receipt reveals](/concepts/what-a-receipt-reveals).
