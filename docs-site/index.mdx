---
title: "Receipts"
description: "ZIP 311 for Orchard and Ironwood, extended so payments can be proven in relation to each other."
---

Receipts proves one Zcash payment without handing over a viewing key. It then goes further: it
proves facts about **sets** of payments (one payer, different payers, one wallet's accounts, a
delegated issuer, a total) without revealing the wallet's history.

## The contrast

<Steps>
  <Step title="Output disclosure: anyone who saw the payment can claim it">
    Reveal an outgoing viewing key's output (what [zreceipt](/reference/related-work) and the
    ZIP 311 output-disclosure draft do) and the verifier learns who was paid and how much. It cannot
    tell the payer from anyone else who saw the payment. The verifier page's *Try a claim by someone
    who only saw it* example shows this: same payment, another key, **NOT PROVEN BY THE PAYER**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spend authority: only the payer can make the receipt">
    A fresh Orchard Action proof for the note the payment spent, plus a signature by the key that
    spent it. This is the part of ZIP 311 that says `TODO` for Orchard. See
    [spend authority](/concepts/spend-authority).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Composability: prove relationships between payments">
    Same payer, different payers, one wallet's sibling accounts, one of a published set of keys, a
    delegated issuer, a total across payments, a threshold treasury. See the
    [overview](/composability/overview).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What each step answers

| Question | Proof | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Did the payer make this payment? | Spend-authority receipt | [Spend authority](/concepts/spend-authority) |
| Did one key make all of these payments? | Same-payer proof | [Same payer](/composability/same-payer) |
| Did N different wallets pay, not one wallet N times? | Payer tags, claim tags | [Distinct payers](/composability/distinct-payers) |
| Are these two payers accounts of one wallet? | Sibling-account proof | [Sibling accounts](/composability/sibling-accounts) |
| Was it signed by one of this department's keys? | Key-set proof | [Key sets](/composability/key-sets) |
| Can a back office issue receipts without my spend key? | Delegation | [Delegation](/composability/delegation) |
| Did I receive at least X across payments? | Income proof | [Income](/composability/income) |
| Was it authorized by this 2-of-3 treasury? | Policy proof | [FROST policy](/composability/frost-policy) |
| Did the money come from this vetted set? | Source and screened-set proofs | [Source of funds](/composability/source-of-funds) |

## Where it stands

Testnet and regtest only, and unaudited. Two of the proofs run on circuits this project
modified or wrote, which no one else has reviewed. [Status](/status) lists each proof with its
evidence and its caveats.
