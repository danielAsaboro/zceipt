Dates are from each project's public commit history, read on 2026-10-22.

| Project | Proves | Does not prove |
|---|---|---|
| [zreceipt](https://github.com/Cosmiq2025/zreceipt) | One output's recipient, amount and memo, opened with the sender's outgoing cipher key, for Sapling, Orchard and Ironwood. Proposed the output-disclosure text for ZIP 311 in zcash/zips#1406 | Who sent the payment. Spend authority exists as a prototype test, not in the product |
| [zcash-delivery-proof](https://github.com/saplingcash/zcash-delivery-proof) | That a transaction delivered one note (receiver, value, memo), checked with no key | Who sent it, and that it was mined |
| [`zcash_disclosure`](https://github.com/auzum197/zcash-disclosure) | Chosen outputs as Bech32m disclosures, a candidate encoding | A signature or spend authority |
| Viewing-key processors (BTCPay plugin, CipherPay) | Everything the key can see | Nothing is withheld |

All of these stop at the part of ZIP 311 that reveals an output. Receipts implements the part
ZIP 311 says makes a disclosure complete: proof that the payer's spend authority made the payment.

## What Receipts adds

1. **Spend authority** for Orchard and Ironwood: a fresh Action proof plus a spend-authorization
   signature, so a receipt can't be made by someone who only watched the payment.
2. **Relationships between payments**: same payer, distinct payers, sibling accounts, key sets,
   delegation, income, threshold policy ([composability](/composability/overview)). None of the
   projects above has any of these.
3. **A chain check**: the verifier fetches the transaction by txid, with a block hint so the
   server sees only a block.
4. **A second verifier** written independently in Python.

## Spend authority, by date

Receipts' first commit on 2026-10-14 creates and verifies the spend-authority proof. zreceipt
added a prototype test of a similar construction on 2026-10-20; its product discloses outputs
only.

The repository's `docs/RELATED-WORK.md` has the full comparison and `docs/COMPARISON.md`
compares viewing-key payment processors.
