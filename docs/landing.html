ZCEIPT · ZIP 311 FOR ORCHARD & IRONWOOD

Prove one Zcash payment. Reveal nothing else.

Shielded Zcash payments are private, which is the point. But sometimes you need to prove one: a shop checking an order, a refund, an exchange clearing a deposit, an auditor. Today that usually means handing over a viewing key, which shows every payment, forever. Zceipt shows just the one.

How it works

  1. 1. Pay as usual from a shielded wallet.
  2. 2. Make a receipt for that one transaction. It shows who was paid, how much and the memo, and carries a zero-knowledge proof that you authorized the payment. Your address, balance and other payments stay hidden.
  3. 3. Share a link. Anyone can check it in their browser, offline if they like. No account, no viewing key, nothing to trust but the math and the chain.
