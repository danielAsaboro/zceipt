Rules

Glass is for chrome and cards. The receipt itself is paper: opaque white, so its numbers never swim.
Blue means proven. Never a decoration, never a general button.
Red means exposed. It appears in one place: the viewing-key beat.
No outlines. Edges come from light (a bright rim on top) and shadow, not from strokes.
