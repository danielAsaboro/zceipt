Receipts · ZIP 311 for Orchard & Ironwood

Prove one Zcash payment. Keep your viewing key.

A receipt shows who was paid, how much and the memo for a single shielded transaction, and proves the sender authorized it. No viewing key changes hands. The proof is checked in this browser; only public chain data is fetched.

How it works

  1. Each disclosed output comes with the one key that opens only that output. Your browser opens it from the transaction on chain and checks the two match.
  2. The payer's wallet makes a fresh zero-knowledge proof that it owned the money the transaction spent, and signs the whole receipt with the same key. Someone who only saw the payment can't make that proof.
  3. A signed message, such as an invoice number or your request's code, ties the receipt to one purpose.
