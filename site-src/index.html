Your wallet is behind frosted glass.

Every shielded payment is sealed on chain: who you paid, how much, the memo, your balance. Nobody can read it. That's the point of Zcash.

Then someone asks for proof

A viewing key shatters all of it.

The shop, the exchange, the auditor. Today the only answer is a viewing key, and it doesn't open one payment. It opens all of them, including the ones you haven't made yet.
